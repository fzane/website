# What Is AI?

*And who does that make us?*

The piece treats "artificial intelligence" as a two-word compound noun and takes each word literally. **Intelligence** means learning from an environment — but which environment matters. **Artificial** mostly means digital, and digital means **copyable**: teach an individual once and you can clone the result endlessly, every copy identical.

## The plane (the central visual)

The whole essay is one diagram, revealed step by step as you scroll. It is a plane with two axes:

- **Vertical axis — intelligence:** from **testable** (bottom, "right answers, an answer key") up to **lived** (top, "no answer key").
- **Horizontal axis — artificial:** from **unique** (left, "one of one") to **copyable** (right, "at any scale").

Four human exemplars are plotted to anchor the vertical axis, all on the unique (left) side:

- **Grandmother** — top-left, "a whole life." Lived, accumulated over time.
- **Gymnast** — middle-left, "a body in the world." Embodied but scorable.
- **Straight-A student** — bottom-left, "a corpus of tests." A fully testable world of questions with known answers.

Make the straight-A student digital and you get **AI**: a copy of the test-taker slid straight across to the bottom-right corner — **copyable × testable**. Copy the gymnast next and you get **robotics**, plotted in the lower-right as "still arriving."

## The four corners

The two axes make four quadrants, and the payoff is which ones are filled:

- **Unique × testable** (bottom-left) — what we have always known.
- **Copyable × testable** (bottom-right) — **AI**.
- **Unique × lived** (top-left) — **us**.
- **Copyable × lived** (top-right) — a question mark. Wisdom at scale. AGI? ASI? Unknown for now.

## The two working corners

The last two scenes swap the plane for a close-up of the two corners that describe the human–AI split:

- **The machine drives** (copyable × testable): a tight cluster of points converging on one center — "best practice, at scale." AI is the correct answer, cloned again and again. It may be standard or generic, but it is right.
- **We navigate** (unique × lived): a wide scatter with a few winners and one point marked "you are here" — "many destinations, most wrong." A unique, lived viewpoint can figure out *where it is worth going*. It might be wrong, but it is different and its own.

## The close

The final panel pairs the two roles as a collaboration: **we navigate** (pick the destination worth reaching — "a vision, off-center, only yours") and **AI drives** (lays the route to it). Like every tool before it, AI takes over something people used to do by hand and, in doing so, poses the question of what the person now does with it. Here the division is specific: the machine supplies the well-worn correct path; the human supplies the choice of where to aim.
